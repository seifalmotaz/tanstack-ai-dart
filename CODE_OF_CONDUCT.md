# Code of Conduct

This is a small community project around an unofficial Dart SDK. Participation
is open to anyone who treats others with basic respect.

## Expected behavior

- Disagree about technical decisions directly and respectfully. Critique code,
  evidence, and tradeoffs, not people.
- Assume good faith and keep discussion focused on the project.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and time zones.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal targeting.
- Spam, promotional noise, or repeated off-topic posting.
- Publishing someone's private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Moderation

Maintainers may moderate, close, or lock abusive or off-topic content, and may
limit participation when needed. Moderation is proportionate and does not
require a formal escalation process.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's "Report content" or "Report abuse" tools on the specific content
or profile, documented at
https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam
For contribution expectations, including AI-assisted work, see
[CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md).
